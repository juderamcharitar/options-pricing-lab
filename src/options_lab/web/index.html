Methodology

The app prices European vanilla contracts with constant rates and dividend yields. The baseline MLP learns normalized call prices from synthetic Black–Scholes labels. Fine-tuning adds an analytical delta target and a soft penalty for violating European price bounds. Put prices use parity. The constrained mixture combines same-forward lognormal prices using positive weights and increasing volatility clocks. Evaluation counts price-bound and Greek sign violations. Simulated hedge performance uses seeded geometric Brownian motion. The numerical benchmark compares a Cox–Ross–Rubinstein tree and two Monte Carlo samplers with the analytic price. The volatility surface generates a synthetic chain and solves for implied volatility at each strike and expiry.
